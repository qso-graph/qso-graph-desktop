# Conditions

Turn it on in **Settings → Extensions**. Conditions shows HF conditions from NOAA's Space Weather
Prediction Center:

- **Solar flux** and the **planetary K and A indices**, each with the time it was measured.
- **NOAA's space weather scales**: radio blackouts (R), solar radiation storms (S) and geomagnetic
  storms (G), with an alert when any is raised.
- **A band-by-band outlook**: each HF band, how it is likely to be, and why.

It asks NOAA at most every 15 minutes, only while the page is open and the computer is online.
