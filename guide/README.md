# QSO Graph Desktop User Guide

QSO Graph Desktop keeps your callsigns, your logins for the services hams use, and the places you
operate from, and puts Parks On the Air, the sun and the moon, band conditions and ADIF's
definitions a click away. Everything you enter stays on your computer.

This guide is short on purpose: a page for each thing you do, in the order you are likely to do it.
Help → User Guide opens it.

## Getting it

- [Install and update](install.md): Windows, Linux and macOS, the portable build, and keeping it
  up to date.

## Getting going

- [Your first steps](getting-started.md): a callsign and its logins, a Location, the reference
  data, and choosing what to add.
- [Home, pins and the rail](home.md): where everything is.

## The pages
