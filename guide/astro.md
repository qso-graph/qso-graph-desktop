# Astro

Turn it on in **Settings → Extensions**. Astro works out where the sun and the moon are for your
active Location, on your computer, so it needs no internet. It reports what the sky is doing and
never predicts.

- **Grey line now**: a world map with the night greyed out, the grey line, the sun and the moon
  where each is overhead, and your Location marked.
- **Grey line DX check**: type a DX station's grid, and see it on the map with the windows in the
  next 24 hours when both ends are in twilight together, in **UTC**, as a contact is logged.
- **The almanac**, for five days: sunrise, sunset, solar noon and the length of the day; civil,
  nautical and astronomical twilight; and the moon's phase, rise and set.
- **Radio depths**, today and tomorrow: when the absorbing D and E layers, and the reflecting F2
  layer, go dark and come back into sunlight.

The almanac's times are local, on your computer's clock, and the page names the time zone the clock
is set to: set your computer's zone, and Astro follows it.
