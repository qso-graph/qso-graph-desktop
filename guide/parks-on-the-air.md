# Parks On the Air

Turn it on in **Settings → Extensions**. It has three tabs. Each filters by POTA's own **Program**,
**Entity** and **Location**, as pota.app's park list does (a filter is shown only when it has more
than one choice), and each park reference opens its page on pota.app.

## Near Me: for activators

The parks within about 300 km of your active Location, a day's drive, nearest first, with how far
each is and which way. **Distance** narrows it to a shorter trip.

It uses the park list you downloaded in **Settings → Reference data**, so it works with no internet
at all, out in the field. Update the list now and then to keep it current.

## Spots: for chasers

Who is on the air from a park right now: the activator, the park, the frequency and mode, and when
they were spotted, newest first. Narrow it by entity, band, mode and location (**My focus area** is
one click). Spots needs the internet.

## Most Wanted

The parks in your [POTA focus area](getting-started.md#6-your-pota-focus-area) that have been
activated least, nearest first: the ones hunters are waiting for, and the ones to put on the air.
It asks POTA for your focus area only, at most once a day for each part of it, so it needs the
internet the first time each day.
