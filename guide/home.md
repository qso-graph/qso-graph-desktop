# Home, pins and the rail

**Home** opens first: a short welcome, your getting-started steps until they are done, and up to
eight **pins**, tiles for the pages you use most. **Customize your pins** chooses them and their
order.

**The rail**, down the left side, has a button for each place: Home, Callsigns, Locations, each
extension you have turned on, and Settings at the foot. It follows your pins' order, and scrolls
if it is full. Each place keeps its own colour.

Some places hold more than one page, shown as **tabs** across the top: Parks On the Air has
**Near Me**, **Spots** and **Most Wanted**. A place opens on the tab you last used.

**The header** shows the time in UTC and locally, and the [Internet lamp](offline.md).

**The status bar**, along the bottom, shows the active callsign and Location, a line when an update
is ready, and **Troubleshooting**.

**Menus:**

- **Setup**: your callsigns, Locations and POTA Focus Area, the reference data, your pins, Settings.
- **Tools**: back up and restore.
- **Help**: what's new, this guide, the project's documents, Troubleshooting.

Dark is the default; **Settings → Appearance** has Light and System.
