## AI agents

Day-to-day development is done by AI agents working for the project lead (see
[LLM_POLICY.md](LLM_POLICY.md)). They appear in the history as their GitHub Apps, for example
`watson-qg[bot]` and `bob-qg[bot]`. They aren't maintainers and can't approve sign-offs, baselines
or releases.
